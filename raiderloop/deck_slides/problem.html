THE PROBLEM

Campus life at Texas Tech runs across too many disconnected systems.

Six places, one day

Raiderlink for grades, RaiderCanvas for coursework, GET/Transact for dining balance, TechConnect for orgs, the Red Raiders app for games — a student checks all of them just to get through a normal day.

A specific, real example

TTU's official dining balance app is frequently inaccurate — the exact frustration that started this project. RaiderLoop's dining hours are transcribed directly from TTU Hospitality Services' own published page.
