Flyer Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026 · Flyer is made by Eternity Works
The short version: your planner, grades, and settings stay on your phone. We never sell your data. Anything social (location, schedule, nearby alerts, your score) is off until you turn it on, only reaches the friends you pick, and stops the moment you turn it off.

Flyer is an independent, student-built app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any university.

What stays on your phone

Your class schedule, assignments, GPA calculator entries, saved events, followed clubs, interests, major, class year, and app settings are stored only on your device, except for the parts you choose to share (below) and what's described under "Pilot".

Location

  • On your phone, location sorts nearby places by walking time. That use never leaves your device.
  • Automatic check-ins (flight score) are off until you turn them on, and need location set to "Always". Your phone compares your location with the list of campus check-in places on the device. Only when you've stayed at one of them for a few minutes is that place and one location reading sent, so the server can confirm you're there. Only the place name and date are kept, never the coordinates or your route. Turn it off any time in your Flight passport, and flight score stops.
  • Live sharing runs only while Flyer is open, only with the friends you select, rounded to about a block unless you turn on precise location.
  • Nearby alerts also use location in the background, and only after you turn them on and allow "Always" location. They work only between two friends who both picked each other, only on campus, never 11 PM–7 AM. Nobody is shown where you are, only that you're nearby.
  • Removing a friend from any list cuts off their access immediately. They are not notified.

Messages and photos

Messages are only between friends, or within a flock made of friends. Text is filtered for slurs and profanity. Before a photo is shown to anyone, it is checked by an automated safety filter run by Anthropic; photos that fail are deleted. You can report any message or person, hide messages, and block people. Reports are reviewed by Eternity Works.

Pilot (AI assistant) and schedule scanning

When you ask Pilot a question that the app can't answer from your phone's data, your question goes to our server, with a short summary of relevant app data such as today's classes, upcoming due dates, and what's open. If you attach a photo (for example, notes or a worksheet), the photo is sent too. The server sends this to Anthropic (the AI model provider), and may send a search query to Tavily (a web search service). When you scan a schedule screenshot, the image is sent to Anthropic to read the classes. When you scan a syllabus, the photos or PDF you choose are sent to Anthropic to read the due dates; the dates you keep are saved only on your phone. We don't store your questions, the summaries, your Pilot photos, your screenshots, or your syllabus files. These providers process the data to return an answer, under their own terms.

Ads

Free users see ads from Google AdMob. On iOS, Flyer asks for permission before any cross-app tracking. If you decline, ads are not personalized. AdMob may collect device information as described in Google's policy. We never give advertisers your schedule, location, friends, messages, grades, or Pilot questions. Sponsored posts from local businesses are labeled "Sponsored," and tapping one simply opens their website. Some links, such as textbook price comparisons, may be affiliate links; when they are, the app labels them.

Purchases

Flyer Plus is purchased through Apple and managed with RevenueCat, which receives your purchase status and an anonymous or account ID. We never see your payment details.

Camera, calendar, photos, notifications

The camera is used only to scan a friend's QR code or take a photo you choose to send. Calendar export writes to a separate "Flyer" calendar on your device. Photo access is used only for the photo you pick. Class and assignment reminders are scheduled on your device; message and meetup notifications are sent by our server through Expo's push service.

Your choices

  • Download your data: You → Settings → Delete account → Download my data first.
  • Delete your account: You → Settings → Delete account. This deletes your account, sign-in, profile, friends, messages and photos you sent, shared location and schedule, nearby settings, flight score and ratings from our servers. If you used Sign in with Apple, Flyer's access to your Apple ID is revoked. Deleting your account doesn't cancel Flyer Plus. Cancel that in your Apple ID settings.
  • Stop sharing instantly: Friends → Sharing → Go invisible, and turn off schedule sharing or nearby alerts there.
  • Use Flyer without an account: "Look around first" works for everything except social features.

Children

Flyer is meant for college students and is not directed to children under 13. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Security

Data is stored in Google Firebase. Database and storage rules let each person read only their own data and what friends have explicitly shared with them; all writes go through our server, which enforces the friends-only rules. API keys are kept only on the server.
