Flyer Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026 · Flyer is made by Eternity Works
The short version: your schedule, assignments, grades, and settings stay on your phone. We never sell your data. Your location is only shared if you turn sharing on, only with friends you pick, and it's deleted when sharing ends.

Flyer is an independent, student-built app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any university.

What stays on your phone

Your class schedule, assignments, GPA calculator entries, saved events, followed clubs, interests, major, class year, and app settings are stored only on your device. They're not uploaded to our servers, except as described under "Pilot" below when you ask a question.

Location

  • Flyer only uses location while the app is open. It never requests background ("Always") location.
  • Location is used on your phone to sort nearby places by walking time. That use never leaves your device.
  • Location is sent to our servers only if you turn on friend sharing. It's visible only to the friends you select. By default it's rounded to about a block; precise location is a separate setting.
  • Removing a friend from your sharing list cuts off their access immediately. They are not notified.

Pilot (AI assistant) and schedule scanning

When you ask Pilot a question that the app can't answer from your phone's data, your question goes to our server. So does a short summary of relevant app data, such as today's classes, upcoming due dates, and what's open. The server sends this to Anthropic (the AI model provider), and may send a search query to Tavily (a web search service). When you scan a schedule screenshot, the image is sent to Anthropic to read the classes. We don't store your questions, the summaries, or your screenshots. These providers process the data to return an answer, under their own terms.

Ads

Free users see ads from Google AdMob. On iOS, Flyer asks for permission before any cross-app tracking. If you decline, ads are not personalized. AdMob may collect device information as described in Google's policy. We never give advertisers your schedule, location, friends, grades, or Pilot questions. Sponsored posts from local businesses are labeled "Sponsored," and tapping one simply opens their website. Some links, such as textbook price comparisons, may be affiliate links; when they are, the app labels them.

Purchases

Flyer Plus is purchased through Apple and managed with RevenueCat, which receives your purchase status and an anonymous or account ID. We never see your payment details.

Calendar, photos, notifications

Calendar export writes to a separate "Flyer" calendar on your device. Photo access is used only for the screenshot you pick. Reminders are scheduled on your device, with no server involved.

Your choices

  • Delete your account: You → Settings → Delete account. This removes your account, profile, friends, location, classmate entry, and Canvas connection from our servers.
  • Stop sharing instantly: Friends → Sharing → Go invisible.
  • Use Flyer without an account: "Look around first" works for everything except friends.

Children

Flyer is meant for college students and is not directed to children under 13. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Security

Data is stored in Google Firebase. Database rules let each person read only their own data and the locations friends have explicitly shared with them. API keys and Canvas tokens are kept only on the server.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and note significant changes in the app. Questions or requests: support@eternityworks.app.
