Flyer Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026 · Flyer is made by Eternity Works LLC
The short version: your schedule, assignments, grades, health readings, and settings stay on your phone. Things you post go only to the people you pick, and most of them disappear on their own. We never sell your data. You can see everything Flyer has about you, and delete it, in the app: You → Settings → What Flyer knows.

Flyer is an independent, student-built app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any university.

What stays on your phone

  • Your class schedule, assignments, GPA entries, saved events, followed clubs, interests, major, class year, syllabus details, the First 30 days checklist, and app settings.
  • Connections: calendar links, Microsoft and Google sign-ins, and an optional Canvas access token are kept in the iPhone Keychain. Flyer talks to those services straight from your phone. They aren't sent to our servers.
  • Apple Health (only if you connect it): last night's sleep, today's steps, and exercise minutes are read on your phone to show next to your schedule. They are never uploaded, saved by us, or shared. Flyer only reads; it never writes to Health.
  • Offline copies of public info (headlines, events, deals) so the app works with a bad signal.

Location

  • Flyer only uses location while the app is open. It never asks for "Always" location.
  • Sorting places by walking time happens on your phone and never leaves it.
  • Location reaches our servers only for features you start: sharing with friends, Flyer Drop's Nearby tab, a Safe Walk, or hide and seek. Each one is described above.

Photos and safety checks

Before a photo or post is shown to anyone, it's checked automatically for things that aren't allowed (for example, nudity, violence, or someone's private information). The text or photo is sent to Anthropic, our AI provider, for that check and isn't kept by them for training. Anything the check isn't sure about is held for a person to review. Hunt photos and building tips can't show people.

Pilot, scanning, and Text Flyer

When you ask Pilot something the app can't answer on its own, your question and a short summary (like today's classes and what's due) go to our server, which sends them to Anthropic and may send a search to Tavily. Scanning a schedule, a syllabus, or an event flyer sends that image or PDF to Anthropic to read it. With Text Flyer on, your texts are delivered by Twilio. We don't store your questions, scans, or texts.

Sharing links, Apple Wallet, and Messages

When you share something, Flyer makes a short link with a preview card so friends without the app can see it. Your Apple Wallet card shows only what your Connect card shows to everyone. Polls you make in the Flyer drawer in Messages live inside the conversation; we only see them if someone without Flyer opens the link, and even then we don't store them.

Browser extension

The Flyer Student Deals extension only looks up a website when you click it, and it sends just that site's name. The optional badge uses a list built into the extension, so your browsing never leaves your computer.

Ads and purchases

Free users see ads from Google AdMob. Flyer asks before any cross-app tracking; if you say no, ads aren't personalized. We never give advertisers your schedule, location, friends, posts, grades, or questions. Sponsored posts are labeled. Flyer Plus is bought through Apple and managed with RevenueCat, which gets your purchase status and an account ID. We never see your payment details.

Your choices

  • See and delete your data: You → Settings → What Flyer knows. Delete one kind of data, or everything.
  • Delete your account: You → Settings → Delete account. This removes everything listed above from our servers.
  • Report or block anyone, or any post, from the ••• or flag on it.
  • Use Flyer without an account: everything except the social features works as a guest.

Children

Flyer is for college students. It isn't directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Security

Data is stored in Google Firebase. Only our servers can write to the database, and its rules let each person read only what's described above. Uploads go to a private folder and are only published after the safety check. Keys and tokens for our providers stay on the server.
