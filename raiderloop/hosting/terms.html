1. What Flyer is

Flyer is an independent, student-built app that collects publicly available campus information in one place. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any university. School names are used only to say which campus the information is about. For anything official (registration, deadlines, grades, emergencies), the university's own systems are the source of truth.

2. Information may be wrong

Hours, events, parking levels, exam times, and Pilot's answers come from public sources and automated tools, and they can be out of date or wrong. Check important details with the official source. Flyer is provided "as is," without warranties. To the extent the law allows, Eternity Works isn't liable for missed classes, deadlines, events, or other losses that come from relying on the app.

3. Emergencies

Flyer is not an emergency service. In an emergency, call 911.

4. Your account and conduct

Keep your sign-in secure. Don't use Flyer to harass, track, or impersonate anyone, or to share someone else's location or information without their consent. Don't try to get around the app's limits or misuse its servers. We may suspend accounts that break these rules.

5. Location sharing

Sharing is optional and only goes to friends you choose. Only share with people you trust, and turn sharing off any time you're not comfortable. You can block anyone; blocked people can't find you, add you, or see your location.

5a. Posts, photos, and community rules

You're responsible for what you post, send, or share in Flyer. There is zero tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users. Don't post or send anything that is sexual, hateful, harassing, violent, or threatening; that shows someone who didn't agree to it; that shares someone's private information; or that's spam or a scam. Hunt photos and building tips must not show people.

Every post and photo is checked automatically before anyone sees it. You can report any post, card, Drop, group, or person, and block anyone. We review reports within 24 hours, remove content that breaks these rules, and remove the accounts of people who post it. Org officer badges mean "confirmed by Flyer," not "official university."

Safe Walk is a way to let friends know you got home. It is not an emergency service and doesn't contact the police: in an emergency, call 911.

6. Flyer Plus

Flyer Plus is an optional auto-renewing subscription billed through your Apple ID. It renews unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period, which you can do in your App Store account settings. Core features stay free.

Flyer shows ads and labeled sponsored posts to stay free. Some links may earn us a commission at no cost to you, and they're labeled when they do. We're not responsible for third-party sites or offers.
