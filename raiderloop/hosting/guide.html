Getting in

Texas Tech guarantees admission based on high school class rank plus an ACT or SAT score. Everyone else gets a full holistic review.

Class rankScore for assured admission
Top 10%No minimum score
Rest of the first quarterACT 24 or SAT 1180
Second quarterACT 26 or SAT 1240
Third quarterACT 27 or SAT 1280
Fourth quarterApplication review
  • Test-optional applications are allowed and reviewed holistically.
  • Holistic review looks at grades and course rigor (Honors, AP, IB), activities, leadership, service and your story. Essays and up to 3 recommendation letters help.
  • Some programs, like business (Rawls) and engineering (Whitacre), only admit students who meet the assured admission table.

Source: TTU Undergraduate Admissions, checked Nov 6, 2026. Always confirm with TTU.

Traditions

Guns Up

The hand sign: thumb up, index finger out like a pistol. You will see it at every game.

The Masked Rider

A student in a black mask and cape rides a black horse onto the field before football games.

Raider Red

The costumed mascot with the oversized mustache and two pistols.

Carol of Lights

Every December, thousands of red, white and orange lights come on across campus at once.

Victory Bells

The bells in the Administration Building ring after big wins and on special days.

Will Rogers and Soapsuds

The statue is wrapped in red crepe paper before home football games.

Life in Lubbock

Lubbock is a West Texas city of more than 250,000 people with big skies, cheap living and a real music history.

Buddy Holly Center

The rock and roll legend was from Lubbock.

Depot District

Live music, restaurants and coffee.

National Ranching Heritage Center

Historic ranch buildings on the edge of campus. Free.

Museum of Texas Tech University

Art, natural history and a planetarium on campus.

Lubbock Lake Landmark

Trails through 12,000 years of history.

Prairie Dog Town in Mackenzie Park

A Lubbock classic. Free.

Science Spectrum

A hands-on science museum with a dome theater.

Cactus Theater

A restored 1938 theater with live shows.
