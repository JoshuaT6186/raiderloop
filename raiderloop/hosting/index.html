Why Flyer exists

Campus life is spread across a dozen websites and apps. Club events live in one place, sports in another, dining in a third, and the official tools are hard to use. Flyer started as a student project called RaiderLoop to fix that, and grew into a full campus app.

It's built and run by a Texas Tech student under Eternity Works. The goal is simple: help students find their people, their classes, and the good stuff happening around them.

Private by design

Your schedule, grades and health readings stay on your phone. Photos are friends-only and checked before anyone sees them. Posts disappear after 24 hours unless you save them. Nothing is sold, ever.
