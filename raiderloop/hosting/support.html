Common questions

How do I make an account?

Open Flyer and tap Let's fly, or go to You, then Settings, then Account. You can use email and a password, or Sign in with Apple on iPhone. You can also look around as a guest first.

Why can't I post or add friends?

Posts, Postcards, friends, Flyer Drop and Group Spaces need a Flyer account, so people know who they're talking to. Guests can use everything else.

Why was my photo not posted?

Every photo is checked before friends see it. Photos with nudity, violence, someone's private info, or (for hunts and building tips) people in them are turned away with a short reason. If the check can't run, the post isn't shared and you can try again in a few minutes.

How do I use Present Mode on a TV?

On the TV or class computer, open this site's /present page. It shows a short code. In Flyer, go to Campus, then Tools, then Present Mode, type the code, and send your slides.

How do I send a Drop to my laptop?

Sign in to the web app on your laptop with the same account. In Flyer on your phone, open Flyer Drop and pick My devices.

Is Flyer an official Texas Tech app?

No. Flyer is an independent, student-built app by Eternity Works LLC. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Texas Tech University.

How do I delete my account and data?

In the app: You, then Settings, then Delete account. That removes your account and everything on our servers. To delete just some data, use You, then Settings, then What Flyer knows. You can also email us and we'll do it for you.

How do I report something or someone?

Tap the flag or the three dots on any post, Drop, card or person, then Report. You can also block anyone from the same menu. We review reports quickly.

Something isn't working

Email us with what you tapped, what happened, and your phone model. Screenshots help a lot.

Delete your data

  1. Open Flyer and go to You.
  2. Tap Settings, then Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Your account, posts, Drops, card, friends and everything else on our servers is removed.

Can't open the app? Email eternityworks2026@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, and we'll delete it for you.
